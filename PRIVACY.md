# SimpPDF Reader Privacy Policy

SimpPDF Reader is a local-first PDF reader.

The app stores imported PDF files and reading metadata locally on your device. This includes document titles, local file names, page counts, last-read page numbers, bookmarks, and notes if you add them in a future version.

SimpPDF Reader does not require an account, does not upload your documents, does not collect document contents, does not track you, does not show ads, and does not use third-party analytics.

Deleting a PDF from the library removes the app's local copy and related local metadata. Resetting the local library removes all imported PDFs and local metadata from the app sandbox.
