SimplePDF Reader Support

SimplePDF Reader is a local-first PDF reader for importing, reading, searching, bookmarking, and sharing PDFs on iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Email: Iam.wei.he@gmail.com

Common Help

  • Import PDFs from the Files app using the import button in Library.
  • Open a PDF from Library to resume reading from the last-read page.
  • Use Search inside the reader to find text in supported PDFs.
  • Use Bookmarks to save important pages locally.
  • Use Settings to reset the local library if needed.

Privacy

SimplePDF Reader stores imported PDFs locally on your device and does not upload your documents.
